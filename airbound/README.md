# AirBound

**Work in progress: not ready for use.** Tested on a single system only, and settings can change between versions.

Secure remote access to Home Assistant from the Companion app and any browser, with no VPN. Choose AirBound's hosted relay (log in with Patreon, 7-day free trial), your own small server, or your router.

Open **AirBound** in the sidebar after starting it, and the wizard walks you through the setup. More at [github.com/oleost/HA-AirBound](https://github.com/oleost/HA-AirBound).
